The House of Veloura

Made slowly. Worn for years.

Veloura is built on a simple belief: the most luxurious thing you can wear is something that feels effortless. We favour clean lines, generous fabrics and craftsmanship you can feel before you see it.

Every collection is designed in small, considered runs, with natural materials chosen for how they age, so each piece becomes more yours with time.
